Auditerra

Land Restoration Monitoring Platform

A two-interface solution designed to connect field experts with smallholder farmers while giving institutional supervisors visibility into land-restoration progress.

View Live Site → View Resources →

Overview

Auditerra combines an independent Flutter mobile application for field experts with a React, Next.js and TypeScript PWA for institutional supervisors.

The platform supports field data collection, farmer ticket management, soil assessment and monitoring of restoration activities across the system.

The Challenge

Land-restoration activities involve multiple stakeholders working across different environments. Field experts need practical tools for collecting information and responding to farmer needs, while institutional supervisors need visibility into activities and progress.

The challenge was to connect these workflows through a practical digital solution.

The Solution

We designed and developed a connected platform that supports field experts through a mobile application while giving supervisors a centralised web interface for monitoring restoration activities.

The solution also integrates a Gemini-powered RAG workflow that combines soil measurements and images with curated Kenyan soil and crop data to generate soil assessments, reports and recommended next actions.
