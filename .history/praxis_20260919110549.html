PRAXIS

Interactive stock market learning through simulation

PRAXIS is an interactive financial market learning platform that teaches beginners how the stock market works by letting them practice it. Learners receive KSh 100,000 in virtual capital, research real companies, make simulated decisions, and reason through outcomes with an AI mentor named Nuru that adapts to their progress. No real money is ever involved.

View Live Site → Read the Case Study

Overview

PRAXIS is a practice environment for the stock market, built for people who have never invested before. It replaces passive financial education (reading articles, watching videos, memorizing definitions) with something closer to a flight simulator: a safe space where you make real decisions, see what happens, and learn from the outcome without risking a shilling.

The core loop is Learn, Research, Decide, Experience, Reflect, Improve. Each cycle ends with the learner explaining why they made a decision, the system showing what actually happened, and Nuru, an AI mentor designed to ask better questions rather than give answers, helping them connect the choice to the outcome.

The product is deliberately not a brokerage, not an investment advice platform, and not a generic finance course. Every screen reinforces that this is simulated, educational, and free of risk.

The Challenge

Someone learning to invest today faces a real gap. They can study every beginner's guide and still freeze the moment a real decision appears. Reading about risk is not the same as experiencing it. The distance between "I understand what a P/E ratio is" and "I know whether this stock is worth my money" is exactly where most beginners stop.

Existing tools do not close this gap. Trading simulators assume you already know the vocabulary. Financial literacy apps test recall rather than build judgment. Brokerage demo accounts can reinforce poor instincts because nothing meaningful is at stake. General AI assistants answer "what should I buy?" directly, which is the opposite of what a beginner needs.

PRAXIS starts from a different premise. The learner makes the decision, explains their reasoning, and sees the result. The mentor's job is to ask the questions that surface what the learner missed. That reflection is the mechanism through which judgment, not just information, is built.

The Solution

PRAXIS is a complete learning platform: landing page, authentication, application shell, dashboard, learning journey, practice library, market explorer with real Kenyan companies, virtual portfolio, progress view, bookmarks, notifications, and a full AI mentor experience.

Learning content is organized across five areas: Market Fundamentals, Risk and Portfolio Construction, Market Mechanics, Company Analysis, and Economic Environment. Each concept is taught through short lessons that lead into a practice scenario where the concept is tested.

The mentor, Nuru, is what makes the product distinctive. It streams responses from Groq with a Gemini fallback, renders financial concepts as live diagrams, generates educational illustrations on request, and offers adaptive quizzes. Every conversation runs through an extraction pass that identifies durable facts about the learner and stores them with vector embeddings so future conversations can retrieve what matters.

Above all, PRAXIS is honest about what it is. The mentor will not tell you what to buy. It will not predict prices. Every screen carries a safety line and every simulated trade is clearly labeled.

Engineering Deep Dive

The mentor pipeline is the technically interesting piece. A user message triggers a chain: embedding the query, retrieving relevant durable facts from PostgreSQL via pgvector, building a system prompt that combines a fixed persona with dynamic context, streaming a completion from Groq, and then a second silent extraction request that pulls durable facts from the turn and persists them.

The rendering pipeline avoids the usual LLM chat pitfalls. Assistant responses are parsed by a hand written markdown renderer that supports a strict whitelist and produces React elements directly. No raw HTML injection on the assistant's own text. Diagrams render with Mermaid's strict security mode. Images are proxied through a server route so the browser never touches the AI provider directly.

Security was treated as a first class concern from the first commit. Every API route re-validates input, sessions live in HttpOnly cookies with a CSRF double submit token, and every external provider call has a timeout and friendly error mapping.
