Auditerra

Land Restoration Monitoring Platform

Auditerra is a connected land-restoration platform that brings together field experts, smallholder farmers and institutional supervisors through a mobile application and web-based monitoring interface.

View Supervisor Dashboard → View Informational Site

Overview

Auditerra was developed as a multi-interface platform for supporting land-restoration work in the field and monitoring progress at an institutional level.

The solution consists of an independent Flutter mobile application for field experts and a React, Next.js and TypeScript PWA for institutional supervisors.

Field experts can capture geolocation and soil-health data, respond to smallholder farmer tickets and document restoration activities. Supervisors can use the web interface to monitor expert locations, farmer tickets, resolution rates, restoration activity and scheduled 60-day revisits.

The Challenge

Land-restoration work involves people collecting information in the field while other stakeholders need a broader view of what is happening across restoration activities.

Field experts need tools that work around their workflows, while institutional supervisors need structured information to monitor activity, follow up on farmer needs and understand progress.

The challenge was to connect these different workflows into one practical system without losing sight of the people using it.

The Solution

We designed Auditerra around two connected experiences. The field application supports experts while they work with farmers, collect environmental information and document restoration activities.

The supervisor PWA provides an institutional view of field activity, allowing supervisors to monitor expert locations, farmer tickets, resolution rates, restoration activities and follow-up visits.

Together, the interfaces create a shared workflow between field-level activity and institutional monitoring.

AI-Powered Soil Assessment

A key part of Auditerra is its soil-assessment workflow. The system combines soil measurements and images with curated Kenyan soil and crop information to provide context-aware assessments.

The Gemini-powered retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) workflow uses this information to generate soil assessments, reports and recommended next actions rather than relying only on a model's general knowledge.

Engineering

The platform combines an independent Flutter mobile application with a React, Next.js and TypeScript progressive web application, supported by backend services and a PostgreSQL database.

The architecture was designed to keep field workflows separate from the institutional monitoring experience while allowing the two interfaces to work with shared application data.
